Disturbances in the facility fall into four distinct categories: visual distortions, auditory anomalies, physical/spatial displacements, and direct entity manifestations. Recognizing the threat tier of an anomaly dictates your immediate reaction—whether you should log the disturbance on your terminal, reset a ward, or immediately retreat to the previous checkpoint.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Jujutsu School Anomaly Shifts

### What should I do if an anomaly appears right behind me during a sweep?
If an anomaly or entity manifests behind you, avoid spinning around erratically. Maintain your heading toward the nearest exit or lighted threshold while listening for audio cues. If the entity is a direct threat, break line of sight immediately, crouch to reduce noise, and deploy a purification talisman if the door ahead becomes sealed.

### How do I know if an anomaly is a real occurrence or a player hallucination?
Check your sanity meter and listen for heartbeat audio pulses. If your sanity is above 75%, any deviation you observe in the academy is an authentic environmental anomaly that must be logged. If your sanity has fallen below 40%, retreat to a cleansed safe room and burn incense before confirming an anomaly report.

### Why do consecutive shifts seem to get faster and more aggressive?
As you progress through shifts, the cursed barrier degrades, causing anomalies to spawn closer together with shorter detection windows. Applying structured **jujutsu school anomaly shift tips** such as assigning dedicated sector check orders and preserving flashlight batteries will help you maintain control during high-intensity late-game hours.

### Can an anomaly appear in the safe room or security office?
In standard difficulty modes, the central security office remains a neutral sanctuary. However, on hard or nightmare shift modifiers, subtle anomalies can appear inside the office itself—such as flickering monitor screens, altered calendar dates, or creeping shadows beneath your desk. Always sweep your home base before logging your final shift report.
