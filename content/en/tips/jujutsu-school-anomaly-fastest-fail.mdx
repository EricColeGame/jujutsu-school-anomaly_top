Passive anomalies—such as a poster changing its portrait subject, a locker door slightly ajar, or an extra step on a short wooden staircase—are entirely harmless to approach. Lethal anomalies, by contrast, possess active particle effects, audible vocalizations, or dynamic movement. If an anomaly moves on its own, it almost always carries an instant-wipe flag upon physical collision.
