## Common Player Traps and How to Avoid Them

Even with comprehensive guides, specific psychological traps cause players to reset their progress repeatedly. Being aware of these pitfalls is a crucial element of our advanced **jujutsu school anomaly anomalies tips**.

### The "Peripheral Mirage" Trap
Human eyes naturally perceive movement in peripheral vision, especially in dark, atmospheric horror settings. Players often mistake natural shadow filtering or slight camera bobbing for an anomaly. Anchor your eyes to stationary geometry—such as doorframes and floor seams—to avoid false alarms.

### The "Late Corridor" Trap
Some anomalies only spawn or activate once you pass the corridor's halfway threshold. A corridor may appear entirely pristine upon entry, but walking past the third set of lockers might trigger a portrait to bleed or an extra locker to open behind you. Always perform a secondary check when reaching the midpoint of the hallway.

### Field of View and Brightness Calibration
Adjusting your display settings can drastically change anomaly visibility:
*   **Brightness:** Keep gamma at the developer's recommended level. Over-brightening the screen washes out subtle color distortions and ghostly silhouettes.
*   **Field of View (FOV):** A FOV that is too wide distorts corridor proportions, making standard wall lengths appear stretched and causing false architectural alarms.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most reliable jujutsu school anomaly anomalies tips for beginners?
The single most effective tip is to treat your first corridor as a study session. Do not rush to complete the loop immediately. Instead, spend several minutes taking note of poster titles, light positions, locker door states, and the background audio tone. ### How do I know if an anomaly is lethal or just a reset?
Most environmental distortions (such as inverted posters, misspelled signs, or floating chairs) simply reset your loop counter if you make the wrong directional choice. However, moving humanoid figures, shadows charging down the hall, and sudden darkness accompanied by heavy breathing are typically lethal. If an entity appears physically active, retreat instantly without hesitation.

### Can an anomaly occur behind me after I enter the corridor?
Yes. Based on player experiences, certain dynamic anomalies only trigger once you reach the midpoint of the hall. If the hallway seems suspiciously clean, quickly turn around before crossing the threshold to the exit. Check whether the door you entered through has vanished or changed appearance.

### Why did my streak reset even though the corridor looked completely normal?
If your run reset on an apparently clean hallway, you likely encountered an auditory anomaly or a subtle ceiling distortion. Audio drops, phantom footsteps, and missing ceiling tiles are frequently missed by players who only focus on eye-level objects like lockers and bulletin boards. Make sure to check ceiling fixtures and listen closely to ambient sound.
