## Anomaly Classification: Curses, Distortions, and Entity Types

In the jujutsu school anomaly night shift, not all threats appear as obvious monsters with claws and fangs. The game tests your cognitive memory by subtly altering props, lighting, audio channels, and spatial layouts. Missing a flipped painting or a missing door handle is just as lethal as failing to outrun an aggressive curse.

According to community reports and compiled player logs, anomalies generally fall into five distinct categories. Recognizing the category instantly determines whether you should file an administrative log, apply a purification seal, or immediately run in the opposite direction.

### Visual and Structural Distortions

Structural anomalies affect the physical architecture of the jujutsu academy. You might enter the second-floor hallway and notice that the corridor extends infinitely into darkness, or that a familiar classroom door now opens into a blank brick wall. These distortions signify spatial tampering caused by innate domain expansions.

### Auditory Anomalies

Auditory cues are often your first and only warning of impending danger. Player experience highlights subtle sounds—such as dripping water where no plumbing exists, phantom footsteps mirroring your cadence, or distant chanting in ancient dialects—as telltale signs that a curse has manifested nearby.

### Entity and Physical Apparitions

Entities represent active supernatural hostiles. Unlike static props, these entities roam the hallways, peer out of locker slats, or pose as mannequins and student silhouettes. Direct eye contact with specific entities can accelerate sanity loss or trigger an immediate lethal chase sequence.

When your sanity dips below 50%, the game begins feeding you false anomalies—objects that appear distorted only to you. If you log a hallucination as a genuine anomaly, the reporting terminal penalizes you with a cooldown timer, leaving actual threats unaddressed.

To restore sanity:

## Advanced Strategies, Audio Cues, and Common Pitfalls

Veteran player experience indicates that approaching the jujutsu school anomaly night shift with a methodical route yields far higher win rates than relying on raw reflexes. The school is engineered to exploit panic; taking a deliberate breath before entering an unlit room often saves your run.

### Master the Audio Spectrum

Audio cues in the academy provide vital clues about what is happening behind closed doors:
- **Low-Frequency Hum:** Indicates a cursed barrier or spatial distortion in the immediate vicinity.
- **Rhythmic Clicking:** Emitted by creeping ceiling entities; look up immediately before walking beneath door lintels.
- **Glass Clinking:** Usually signifies that a display case in the artifact gallery has shattered, liberating a minor cursed object.

### Avoid Common Rookie Mistakes

1. **Over-Reporting Uncertain Anomalies:** Submitting false flags to the reporting console incurs heavy penalty locks. If you are not at least 90% sure a prop moved, cross-reference your baseline photos before hitting submit.
2. **Ignoring Battery Depletion:** Operating your flashlight at low charge causes it to flicker, triggering rapid sanity degradation and obscuring subtle visual cues. Always swap batteries in safe rooms before they drop below 20%.
3. **Sprinting Everywhere:** Running drains stamina, makes immense noise, and accelerates sanity loss when turning sharp corners into dark spaces. Walk methodically and sprint only to escape active pursuits.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Night Shift

### How long does a jujutsu school anomaly night shift take in real time?
A standard shift running from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM typically lasts between 20 to 25 minutes of real-world gameplay. However, pausing in safe rooms, managing prolonged hide sequences during entity hunts, or experiencing terminal lockdown delays can extend the shift closer to 30 minutes.

### What should I do if an anomaly report comes back as rejected?
If your report is rejected during the jujutsu school anomaly night shift, you are either misclassifying the phenomenon (e.g., calling an auditory mimic a spatial distortion) or suffering from low-sanity hallucinations. Retreat to a safe room, drink herbal tea to stabilize your mental meter, and reinspect the sector with your UV light.

### Can all anomalies be banished using talismans?
No. Talismans and purification seals only work against minor curses, possessed inanimate objects, and broken containment barriers. High-grade apparitions and structural distortions cannot be purged with items; they must be logged correctly at the terminal or evaded until the morning temple bells ring.

### Does the school map randomize on every shift?
Yes. While the general architectural blueprint (the core four wings) remains consistent, room configurations, prop placements, anomaly spawns, and entity patrol routes are procedurally generated for every new shift, ensuring no two runs feel identical.
