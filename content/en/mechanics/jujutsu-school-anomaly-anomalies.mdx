## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are jujutsu school anomaly anomalies and how do they function?
In the context of the game, **jujutsu school anomaly anomalies** refer to environmental corruptions, ghostly entity spawns, and spatial distortions caused by cursed energy within the school grounds. Players must spot these irregularities to decide whether to advance through the corridor or turn around to keep their survival loop counter progressing.

### Are all jujutsu school anomaly anomalies fatal if approached?
No, not all anomalies cause instant death. Many anomalies are purely environmental or sensory clues, such as shifted posters, altered shadows, or misplaced items. However, aggressive entities or spatial traps will trigger an immediate defeat or force a full loop counter reset if approached carelessly.

### How can I distinguish subtle anomalies from normal room variations?
The most reliable technique is memorizing the baseline corridor during loop zero. Take a mental snapshot of key landmarks: the number of lockers, the content of wall posters, lantern brightness, and ambient sounds. Any deviation from this pristine baseline must be treated as an anomaly.

### What should I do if jujutsu school anomaly anomalies fail to clear after turning back?
Community reports suggest that if an anomaly seems persistent across multiple cycles, ensure you are fully crossing the rear doorway threshold to properly register the retreat. Lingering in the doorway can prevent the game engine from cycling to the next generated hallway state.
