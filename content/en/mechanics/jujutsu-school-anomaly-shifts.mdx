## Identifying Anomaly Types During Active Shifts

To effectively purge irregularities, you must understand what you are hunting. Recognizing subtle deviations during active **jujutsu school anomaly shifts** prevents instant failure states caused by overlooked environmental corruption. 

Anomalies generally split into five major categories:

1. **Object Alterations:** Everyday classroom or training gear appearing in impossible positions, multiplying, or vanishing.
2. **Structural & Spatial Warps:** Corridors expanding in length, missing stairwells, inverted ceiling rafters, or sealed doors where open pathways belong.
3. **Auditory & Cursed Frequency Phantasms:** Whispering vents, phantom footfalls on tatami mats, discordant bell tolls, or reversed voice audio on radio channels.
4. **Lighting & Visual Distortions:** Unnatural blood-red moonlight, lingering dark silhouettes, missing reflection profiles in washroom mirrors, or camera feed static.
5. **Entity Invasions:** Physical manifestations of wandering curses, possessed practice dummies, or mimetic figures posing as instructors or fellow students.

| Anomaly Class | Visual / Audio Indicator | Detection Difficulty | Threat to Sanity | Mandatory Counter-Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Translocation** | Desks, scrolls, or weapons moved to odd rooms | Low | Negligible | Log precise sector and original coordinate |
| **Architecture Warp** | Hallways infinitely looping or missing exit doors | Medium | Moderate | Apply directional chalk / refresh barrier seal |
| **Acoustic Mirage** | Chanting behind walls; delayed footsteps | Medium | Low | Verify via audio sensor; reset audio feed |
| **Shadow Mimic** | Silhouettes without physical light sources | High | High | Expose with blessed flashlight; log shadow anomaly |
| **Curse Manifest** | Ooze on tatami, floating talismans, stalker figures | Extreme | Lethal | Deploy exorcism ward immediately; evacuate sector |

When an entity anomaly goes unchecked for more than one phase cycle, it anchors into that wing of the school. Once anchored, it permanently blocks security feeds and forces you to perform dangerous manual clears on foot.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Jujutsu School Anomaly Shifts

### What causes jujutsu school anomaly shifts to increase in difficulty?
Shift difficulty scales primarily based on in-game time progression and unresolved anomalies from preceding cycles. If an investigator misses a translocation or auditory warp in Shift 1 or 2, that lingering cursed energy bleeds into the environment. This accelerates the spawn timer of aggressive entities during subsequent **jujutsu school anomaly shifts**, forcing players to confront both old distortions and new incursions simultaneously.

### How do I distinguish between a false hallucination and a real anomaly?
Hallucinations are personal status effects caused by low mental focus or prolonged exposure to high cursed energy zones. They typically manifest as flashing peripheral figures, sudden whispering directly in your audio channels, or temporary screen shaking. Real anomalies permanently affect the room geometry or physical assets until officially logged or cleansed. Check your sector reference logs or have a teammate verify the room to confirm the disturbance.

### Can you skip or accelerate jujutsu school anomaly shifts?
No, shifts cannot be skipped entirely because they operate on an immutable temporal cycle. However, clearing all active environmental anomalies in a sector before the midpoint of a shift stabilizes the cursed energy gauge. This limits aggressive entity incursions and allows investigators to enter the next phase with a clean baseline, significantly reducing the difficulty spike of later rounds.
