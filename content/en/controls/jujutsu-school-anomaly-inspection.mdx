## Understanding the Core Inspection Mechanics

At its foundation, anomaly inspection tasks players with scanning recurring zones—such as classroom hallways, courtyard shrines, and training grounds—to detect subtle deviations caused by cursed energy leaks. An anomaly can be visual, auditory, spatial, or behavioral. Failing to report or cleanse an anomaly before proceeding resets your sector progress or triggers a sudden ambush.

Inspecting an anomaly is not just about looking around; it requires activating distinct sensory focus modes. When you initiate an inspection state, your character slows down, camera sensitivity sharpens, and your cursed flow highlights environmental irregularities. Managing this state effectively demands ergonomic keybindings that do not lock your movement fingers while analyzing micro-details.

| Inspection Component | Primary Function | Ideal Input Binding | Risk Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cursed Sight (Scan)** | Reveals residual cursed aura and hidden wall markers | `Hold Right Click` / `LT (L2)` | Drains stamina/focus; restricts sprint |

| **Log Entry / Mark** | Records confirmed anomalies to seal the current sector | `R` / `Y (Triangle)` | False reports destabilize sector containment |
| **Emergency Step-Back** | Quickly breaks focus mode to evade sudden spatial shifts | `Space + S` / `B + Back (Circle)` | High stamina cost |

Community reports consistently emphasize that overusing Cursed Sight can cloud your standard vision with residual blue-violet glare. Balancing natural observation with short bursts of inspection focus is the most reliable way to maintain full spatial awareness.

### Key Sensitivity and Visual Settings

*   **Mouse DPI / Sensitivity:** Set your camera sensitivity to a low-medium range (400–800 DPI with balanced in-game smoothing). High sensitivity can cause you to skip past faint hairline fractures or distorted shadows during slow pans.
*   **Field of View (FOV):** Maintain an FOV between 90 and 100 degrees. Exceeding 105 degrees introduces fish-eye distortion at screen edges, making normal hallway architecture appear unnaturally curved and triggering false anomaly flags.
*   **Motion Blur & Film Grain:** Completely disable motion blur, depth of field, and film grain in your display options to ensure all structural textures remain crisp.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if an anomaly doesn't trigger a visual or audio cue?
Some high-tier anomalies alter conceptual or behavioral states, such as frozen wall clock hands or subtle changes to window reflections. If standard senses show nothing yet the room feels off, inspect environmental lighting, shadow angles, and mirror surfaces.

### Can I complete an anomaly inspection run using default keybindings?
Yes, default controls are functional for early stages. However, remapping sensory focus and interact commands to independent fingers or mouse buttons drastically reduces mechanical delays, which becomes vital during late-tier sectors where evasion windows shrink to fractions of a second.

### Why does my cursed sight pulse fail during certain inspections?
Certain Class 4 spectral anomalies emit a dampening field that jams cursed energy techniques. When this occurs, your interface will flicker or your scan will fizzle. Rely exclusively on physical baseline checks and natural observation until you cross beyond the dampener's radius.

### What is the penalty for submitting a false anomaly inspection report?
Submitting an incorrect report destabilizes the sector's barrier integrity. In most runs, three false reports will trigger an immediate barrier collapse, summoning an unavoidable curse entity and resetting your current corridor milestone.
