## Core Controls and Console Interface for the Security Guard

Operating your surveillance station effectively requires memorizing split-second switch keys and navigation hotkeys. As the night progresses, anomaly frequencies spike, giving you narrower reaction windows to log deviations and cycle through school wings.

The console interface acts as your primary command hub. From here, players alternate between stationary desk operations—such as toggling camera feeds, switching monitor filters, and triggering containment wards—and physically patrolling the corridors. Community reports emphasize that mastering camera shortcuts reduces response times by nearly half compared to manual mouse-clicking across monitor panels.

The table below outlines the standard control layout across standard keyboard and mouse setups alongside gamepad configurations.

| Action | Primary PC Input | Gamepad (Xbox / Generic) | Context / Function |

| **Move Forward / Backward** | `W` / `S` | Left Stick (Up / Down) | Hallway and courtyard patrol |

### Surveillance Desk Navigation

When seated at the security terminal, your inputs shift from standard first-person movement to system manipulation. You must smoothly swap between monitor quadrants to detect subtle environmental shifts, such as misplaced training dummies, inverted talismans, or flickering corridor lighting. 

Key combinations like `1` through `8` correspond to specific wings: the Training Grounds, Dormitory Hallways, Headmaster’s Corridor, Library of Cursed Relics, and the Basement Shrine. Quick-swapping using number keys rather than cycling linearly ensures you can verify an anomaly reported across distant sectors before the spirit relocates.

### Flashlight, Stamina, and Patrol Movement

Leaving the console cabin requires careful stamina management. Sprinting produces audible footfalls that attract roaming wandering curses. Toggling your flashlight with `F` is necessary to spot micro-distortions on physical walls, but leaving the beam active when turning blind corners can instantly aggravate lurking entities. 

Player experience suggests tapping sprint in three-second bursts during hallway transit to retain enough stamina meter for emergency evasions.

## Security Guard Patrol Protocol: Identifying School Anomalies

Serving as the **jujutsu school anomaly security guard** demands methodical categorization. Anomalies range from minor architectural displacement to aggressive grade-level curses manifesting right in front of security lenses. 

Misidentifying an anomaly or filing a false report incurs penalty points, which destabilize the school's defensive barrier. Below is an operational breakdown of common anomaly classes and the required control input sequence for each.

| Anomaly Class | Visual & Audio Indicators | Severity Level | Immediate Response Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Object Displacement** | Rearranged desks, floating scrolls, rotated statues | Low | Target object, hold `E` to verify, press `R` to file report |
| **Audio Distortion** | Whispering vents, reversed chanting, phantom steps | Medium | Access Sound Mixer panel (`M`), isolate audio channel, log sector |
| **Structural Warping** | Lengthened hallways, bleeding walls, inverted doors | Medium | Cycle back to adjacent camera, confirm distortion, press `Spacebar` |
| **Shadow / Silhouette** | Unregistered figures standing in window frames or rafters | High | Do NOT shine direct flashlight; switch to Ward view (`Q`) and log |
| **Active Spirit Incursion** | Broken camera static, direct lens obstruction, red aura | Critical | Immediately seal sector (`LT`/`Q`), evacuate desk if in proximity |

### Camera Discipline and Sweep Routes

A structured sweep routine prevents critical sectors from sitting unmonitored for too long. Experienced guards recommend a strict five-second-per-feed rule:

1. **Feed 01–03 (Entry & Courtyard):** Check for outdoor apparitions and broken barrier wards.
2. **Feed 04–06 (Main Hallways & Classrooms):** Verify desk counts and blackboard inscriptions.
3. **Feed 07–08 (Shrine Vault & Storage):** Watch for leaking cursed energy or displaced sealing cloths.

If any feed exhibits static, avoid mashing camera buttons. Static indicates an active presence crossing the sensor threshold; immediately check adjacent feeds to track the entity's vector.

## Troubleshooting Input Latency and Controller Configuration

Sluggish controls can be fatal when an anomaly requires an instant response. Many players report occasional frame drops or input lag when transitioning from first-person walking to terminal monitor viewing. Optimizing your configuration ensures your actions register immediately during high-stress encounters.

| Setting | Recommended Value | Impact on Gameplay and Control Responsiveness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mouse Sensitivity** | Medium-Low (800–1000 DPI) | Smoother inspection sweeps; prevents overshooting micro-anomalies |
| **Field of View (FOV)** | 90° – 100° | Expands peripheral sightlines in dark corridors without fisheye distortion |
| **V-Sync** | Disabled (or Fast Sync) | Eliminates mouse lag on monitor consoles; critical for quick-swapping |
| **Motion Blur** | Disabled | Keeps details sharp while turning quickly during spirit evasion |
| **Gamepad Deadzone** | 0.05 – 0.08 | Eliminates stick drift while maintaining micro-adjustments for aiming |

If you experience sticky inputs while cycling feeds as the **jujutsu school anomaly security guard**, check if your keybinding configuration has overlapping commands between UI selection and physical action keys. Binding your "Log Anomaly" key strictly to `Spacebar` while keeping `E` solely for physical interaction prevents accidental logs when simply trying to open your security station's door.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most important controls to master first as the jujutsu school anomaly security guard?
The absolute priority is mastering camera hotkeys (`1`–`8`) alongside your report shortcut (`Spacebar` or `R`). Being able to cycle directly to a compromised room and file the anomaly within two seconds is essential for keeping school barrier integrity intact.

### How do I escape if an entity enters the security office?
Do not try to hide beneath the desk. Press `Q` to trigger the terminal ward if power is available, hit `E` to stand up immediately, and run through the side fire exit rather than down the main hallway. Keep your flashlight turned off until you break line of sight.

### Why is my anomaly report marked as incorrect even when something moved?
Some anomalies manifest as phantom illusions designed to bait false alarms. If a moved object shifts back to its original location within three seconds of looking at it, it is a mirage anomaly. Reporting a mirage as a physical displacement anomaly will incur an accuracy penalty.

### Can I play the jujutsu school anomaly security guard role using a controller?
Yes, modern gamepads are fully supported. Use the bumpers (`LB`/`RB`) to cycle camera feeds and the right trigger (`RT`) to log anomalies. Ensure you adjust your stick deadzones in settings to maintain snappy camera control during high-speed hallway chases.
