## Troubleshooting Common Input Latency and Glitches

Occasionally, players encounter frustrating control anomalies that stem from system latency or software conflicts rather than in-game curses. Addressing these technical issues early ensures that every input is registered accurately.

Use the troubleshooting checklist below to identify and fix common input responsiveness problems:

| Issue Encountered | Probable Cause | Recommended Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Delayed Key Registration** | V-Sync buffering or high render latency | Disable V-Sync in graphics settings; cap framerate manually |
| **Ghost Running / Stuck Keys** | Overlay software or polling rate conflict | Disable background discord/game overlays; set mouse to 1000Hz |
| **Gamepad Not Detected** | Driver conflict or secondary virtual controller | Unplug secondary peripherals; launch in dedicated controller mode |
| **Camera Spinning Infinitely** | Uncalibrated thumbstick deadzone or virtual joystick | Increase inner deadzone slightly in the controller settings menu |
| **Missed Touch Tap Events** | Screen multi-touch limits or oily screen surface | Clean screen and enable developer multi-touch visualization |

Refining your software environment guarantees that missed anomaly reports are the result of gameplay challenges, not input lag. Once your hardware and keybinds are calibrated to your natural playstyle, logging paranormal phenomena becomes an intuitive, second-nature process.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I fully remap my jujutsu school anomaly controls on PC?
Yes, the PC version supports remapping through the in-game options menu under the Controls or Keybinds tab. Players can easily swap key assignments for interaction, cursed senses, sprinting, and anomaly logging to match their personal layout preferences or mouse button configurations.

### What should I do if my jujutsu school anomaly controls feel sluggish on controller?
If your controller feels unresponsive, access the game's input settings to lower the inner deadzone to roughly 0.05 and turn off camera smoothing. Additionally, ensure that your TV or monitor is running in low-latency "Game Mode" to eliminate display input delay.

### Is it better to set sprint to hold or toggle in jujutsu school anomaly?
Most experienced players recommend using "Hold to Sprint" on PC to prevent accidental stamina depletion during quiet exploration. On gamepads and mobile devices, however, "Toggle Sprint" is generally preferred to reduce thumb strain during lengthy corridor investigations.

### How do I report anomalies quickly without getting caught by cursed spirits?
Rebind your report key to an easily accessible secondary button, such as `Q` on PC or a rear paddle on your controller. This allows you to verify and submit anomaly reports without taking your fingers off your movement keys, letting you back away from threats simultaneously.
