## Optimal Combos and Tactical Rotations

Maximizing damage with **jujutsu school anomaly gojo** requires chaining knockbacks into vacuum pulls before finishing with high-impact bursts. Because basic light-attack strings deal modest damage on their own, skills must be buffered during attack recovery frames.

### Standard Ground Pressure Chain
The most reliable combo string for standard skirmishes relies on close-range stagger into Lapse: Blue. Start with three consecutive light attacks (M1), activate Teleport Strike behind the opponent to bypass front-facing parries, and immediately cancel into Lapse: Blue. Once the enemy is pulled into your hitbox, unleash an uncharged Reversal: Red into an environmental wall.

### Aerial Reset and Finisher String
When launching an opponent into the air, follow with an air dash and two light aerial strikes. Buffer your secondary Teleport to position above the falling target, spike them down toward the arena floor, and charge a short-burst Hollow: Purple as they recover from the knockdown state.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective way to manage Cursed Energy on Gojo?
Avoid keeping the Infinity barrier toggled indefinitely. Toggle it dynamically right before enemy strikes land, and rely on base movement speed to dodge slow projectile attacks. This conserves your energy pool for offensive abilities like Lapse: Blue and Reversal: Red.

### How do you counter opponents with barrier-piercing weapons?
When facing opponents armed with barrier-negating items, treat your defense as conventional. Utilize back dashes, high-speed aerial movement, and spacing tools to bait missed swings, then immediately punish with a fast Teleport Strike.

### Can Unlimited Void be canceled by other domain expansions?
Yes. If an opposing player activates their Domain Expansion simultaneously or possesses higher domain refinement stats, a domain clash occurs. In domain clashes, meter management and rapid input sequences dictate which barrier overpowers the other.

### Is jujutsu school anomaly gojo suitable for beginners?
While **jujutsu school anomaly gojo** offers immense defensive protection through Infinity, managing energy expenditure, cooldown timers, and long cast animations demands moderate game sense. New players should practice combo links in training modes before entering competitive ranked playlists.
