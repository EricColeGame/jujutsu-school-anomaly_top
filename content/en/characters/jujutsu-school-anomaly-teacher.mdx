## Understanding the Jujutsu School Anomaly Teacher Archetype

Faculty members in the academy are supposed to act as guardians and guides, offering safe zones and occasional mission briefings. However, when an anomaly manifests, it warps familiar mentor figures into lethal predators. A **jujutsu school anomaly teacher** mimics the silhouette, voice, and uniform of respected sorcery instructors—such as the blindfolded special-grade mentor or the stern puppet-crafting principal—while masking malevolent intent.

The core challenge stems from perception. Unlike standard wandering spirits that produce obvious visual static or aggressive screams, a teacher entity operates through deception. It will stand near chalkboards, pace outside dormitories, or call your name from empty offices. Recognizing the minute differences between legitimate non-player mentors and hostile mimics is your primary survival check.

### Detailed Breakdown of Major Variants

#### 1. The Blindfolded Mimic
By far the most notorious encounter, this entity mimics the academy’s strongest instructor. It hovers slightly off the ground, often appearing at the end of long, narrow dorm corridors. When approaching this variant, player experience suggests keeping your flashlight off. The entity reacts violently to direct artificial illumination, closing gaps with teleport-like burst speed.

#### 2. The Inverted Proctor
Found primarily inside standard lecture halls and the primary testing arena, the Inverted Proctor appears to be grading papers or writing on whiteboards. The primary anomaly sign is physical orientation: its hands may be reversed, or its head will twist beyond normal human anatomy to track your movements while its torso remains still. 

#### 3. The Hallway Overseer
This rare manifestation commands entire floor wings. When the Hallway Overseer occupies a zone, standard room numbers scramble into unreadable symbols. Surviving this encounter requires immediate evasion, as prolonged exposure drains sanity and triggers lethal shadow tendrils from surrounding lockers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first when a jujutsu school anomaly teacher spots me?
Break direct eye contact immediately and locate the nearest corner or doorway. Running in a straight line down long corridors gives high-tier anomalies an easy path to close the distance. Deploy a Cursed Barrier Tag across a door frame to buy time, then navigate through adjoining classrooms to lose line of sight.

### How can I distinguish between a real mentor and a jujutsu school anomaly teacher?
Check physical shadows and facial textures. Authentic teachers cast accurate, single shadows and have smooth animations. An anomalous teacher will often lack a shadow, have reversed or untextured eyes, speak in glitched or reversed audio loops, and pace with an erratic, unnatural cadence.

### Can you eliminate a jujutsu school anomaly teacher using cursed weapons?
In current game versions, teacher-class entities cannot be permanently defeated using standard weapons. Cursed tools will temporarily stun or stagger the anomaly for several seconds, but your goal should always be completing room verification, logging the anomaly at the security terminal, and surviving until the shift cycle resets.

### Does the jujutsu school anomaly teacher spawn on every floor?
No. Faculty anomalies spawn according to the current corruption index of the facility. While low-tier rooms may only feature minor environmental distortions (like floating desks or misplaced scrolls), faculty-level teacher anomalies typically begin patrolling once your shift reaches higher corruption thresholds or after midnight events trigger.
