## Core Archetypes of Jujutsu School Anomaly Students

Within the anomaly containment zones, sorcerer trainees are divided into tactical archetypes based on their innate technique, cursed energy efficiency, and physical conditioning. No single student can handle every breach alone; balanced teams rely on distinct roles to establish domain counter-measures, absorb frontal assault damage, and execute exorcisms before cursed energy depletion sets in.

### The Four Primary Student Roles

1. **Vanguard Strikers**: Frontline combatants who utilize close-quarters combat (Taijutsu) infused with raw cursed energy to hold boss aggro and execute crushing critical strikes.
2. **Barrier Technicians**: Tactical casters responsible for erecting Simple Domains, curtains (screens), and defensive wards that protect squishier teammates from lethal environmental debuffs.
3. **Cursed Tool Specialists**: Versatile weapon masters who leverage enchanted blades, polearms, and chains to bypass anomalous damage resistances without exhausting personal energy reserves.
4. **Support & Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT) Users**: The backbone of prolonged expeditions, capable of converting negative cursed energy into positive healing energy to restore damaged limbs and purge mental hexes.

## Stat Priorities and Cursed Energy Management

Building effective **jujutsu school anomaly students** requires deliberate point distribution. Cursed energy is a finite resource during anomaly delves, meaning wasted output frequently leads to combat burnout.

```
Total Combat Rating = (Cursed Output × 0.40) + (Efficiency × 0.35) + (Resilience × 0.25)
```

Understanding how stats interact ensures your student does not run dry during extended multi-wave engagements.

### Critical Attributes Explained

- **Cursed Output (CE Output)**: Determines the maximum force behind your physical blows and offensive techniques. High output causes rapid target posture breaks.
- **Energy Reserves (CE Pool)**: The total volume of energy available before your character experiences exhaustion or technique burnout.
- **Flow Efficiency**: Decreases the active cost of continuous reinforcement and domain upkeep.
- **Physical Resilience**: Governs baseline health, stagger resistance, and recovery speed following knockdowns.

## Best Practices for Surviving High-Grade Anomalies

Surviving complex anomaly chambers requires discipline, communication, and sharp resource management. Adhere to these proven tactical rules during deep expeditions:

- **Monitor Technique Burnout**: After deploying a maximum output technique or domain defense, your innate technique temporarily enters cooldown. Switch to basic physical parrying or let your frontline tank absorb pressure.
- **Maintain Barrier Geometry**: If your squad relies on a Barrier Technician's Simple Domain, stay strictly within its boundary circle. Stepping outside exposes you to the enemy's sure-hit domain effects immediately.
- **Coordinate Stagger Gauges**: Anomalies possess an internal balance meter. Save your hardest-hitting physical attacks for when the boss is staggered to trigger vulnerability multipliers.
- **Manage Energy Reserves**: Never drop below 20% maximum cursed energy during exploration phases. Random ambush spawns can easily punish depleted trainees.

By adhering to these fundamentals, your squad of **jujutsu school anomaly students** can consistently overcome overwhelming cursed odds and clear the academy's most hazardous sectors.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best starting stats for jujutsu school anomaly students?
Beginners should focus on a 2:1 ratio between Flow Efficiency and Physical Resilience before putting heavy investment into CE Output. Building a strong baseline of stamina and damage reduction keeps you alive while you learn the attack patterns and parry timings of early anomaly types.

### Can jujutsu school anomaly students clear Special Grade encounters solo?
While solo clears are technically possible, they require an end-game build—such as an optimized Heavenly Restriction Berserker or an S-Tier Spatial Weaver—equipped with anti-domain defenses. For the vast majority of players, team play is necessary to handle multi-layered mechanics and guaranteed-hit attacks.

### How does technique burnout affect student characters in combat?
Technique burnout occurs immediately after casting high-drain abilities or barrier expansions. During this window, your special cursed abilities are temporarily locked, forcing you to rely solely on martial arts, basic cursed tool attacks, and physical evasions until your flow stabilizes.

### Which student role is most critical for multiplayer anomaly raids?
The Barrier Technician is widely considered the most indispensable role in co-op runs. Without a player capable of deploying Simple Domain or defensive barriers, the entire party remains exposed to fatal domain attacks deployed by high-grade anomaly bosses.
