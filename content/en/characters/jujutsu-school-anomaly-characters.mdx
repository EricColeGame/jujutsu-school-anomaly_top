## Pro Tips for Dominating Combat Encounters

To separate yourself from casual players, integrate these community-verified tactical habits into your regular gameplay sessions:

*   **Manage the Anomaly Meter:** Lingering too long in corrupted classroom zones rapidly fills your sanity corruption bar. When full, your controls invert and cursed techniques cost double energy. Rotate out of contaminated zones periodically.
*   **Master the Deflect Timing:** Unlike standard blocking, which drains your stamina guard meter, a timed deflect completely negates incoming damage and opens up humanoid anomalies for an immediate combo starter.
*   **Conserve CE for Defensive Flashes:** Avoid emptying your entire cursed reserve during an attack flurry. Always reserve at least 25% of your energy pool to execute an emergency evasive blink when unexpected floor hazards trigger.
*   **Cycle Team Cooldowns:** Coordinate with your squad to alternate stun abilities rather than stacking them simultaneously, preventing diminishing crowd control returns against resilient cursed spirits.
