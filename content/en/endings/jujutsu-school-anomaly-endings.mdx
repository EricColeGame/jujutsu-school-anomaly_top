## Strategic Tips for Navigating Anomaly Loops

Consistently discovering all branching paths requires methodical habits. Relying strictly on sight can be misleading, as several supernatural phenomena in the school manifest through directional sound, floor vibration, or subtle chronological shifts on wall clocks.

- **Establish a Fixed Inspection Pattern:** Scan your surroundings in a counter-clockwise circle upon entering every loop: floorboards, left lockers, ceiling beams, classroom door, right wall frames, and finally the exit doorway.
- **Wear Quality Headphones:** Roughly 30% of anomalies give off audio cues before becoming visually apparent. Distinct indicators include dragging chalk, floorboard creaks out of sync with your footsteps, and breathing behind locker vents.
- **Watch the Clock Hands:** The wall clock above the exit displays 00:00. If the second hand moves backward, jumps two seconds at once, or freezes entirely, you are dealing with a temporal anomaly.
- **Manage Sprinting Conservatively:** Running increases your detection radius for audio-based curses. Walk whenever inspecting subtle anomalies to preserve stamina in case a sudden pursuit event triggers.
- **Document Loop Anomalies:** Keep a physical notepad or quick reference sheet handy. Certain anomalies appear only once per save file, making instant recognition crucial during advanced runs.

By adopting this disciplined approach, you can systematically catalog anomalies, preserve your mental resistance, and trigger each distinct finale without tedious repetition.
