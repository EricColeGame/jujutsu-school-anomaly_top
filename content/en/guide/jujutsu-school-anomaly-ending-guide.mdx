## Strategies to Avoid Bad Endings and Looping Pitfalls

Failing a run in the later sectors can be immensely frustrating, especially when an anomaly blends seamlessly into the architecture. Applying targeted gameplay habits will keep your streak alive and steer you away from sudden game-over screens.

### 1. Maintain Consistent Movement Pacing
Rushing through corridors is the primary cause of missed anomalies. Always enter a hallway at walking speed. Sprinting should only be used when an active, aggressive anomaly pursues you toward the exit doors. Rushing past a subtle wallpaper tear or miswritten talisman sends you straight back to Corridor 0.

### 2. Audio Calibration Is Vital
Over 30% of high-loop anomalies in the game rely on directional sound cues. Playing with stereo headphones provides a marked tactical advantage. If you hear a faint sliding door sound behind you or rhythmic footsteps out of sync with your own, you are in an anomalous sector.

### 3. Beware the "False Normal" Trap
According to player experience reports, one of the trickiest mental traps occurs when two completely normal corridors generate in a row. Players often assume the second corridor *must* contain an anomaly and second-guess standard decorations. Trust your baseline memory: if nothing has changed, confidently walk forward.
