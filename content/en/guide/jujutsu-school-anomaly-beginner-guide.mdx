Even dedicated players stumble when transitioning from basic campus tasks to high-tier anomaly breaches. Avoiding these routine missteps will keep your progression smooth:

By applying these disciplined habits and staying composed during chaotic breaches, you will swiftly master the academy grounds and climb the ranks of elite sorcerers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most reliable starter technique for beginners?
The Striker archetype is widely regarded as the most accessible choice for novices. Its straightforward melee combos, high guard-break capability, and low resource overhead allow new players to focus on mastering defensive parries and enemy mechanics without managing complex ability rotations.

### How do I recover cursed energy quickly in the middle of a battle?
Cursed energy recovers naturally over time, but you can accelerate this process by landing basic light melee attacks on opponents. If you create distance, you can also use your channeled focus stance, though you must ensure no nearby anomalies can interrupt your meditation.

### Why do I run out of stamina so quickly according to this jujutsu school anomaly beginner guide?
Stamina drains rapidly if you continuously sprint and spam your evasive dash. To manage your stamina efficiently, use your dash strictly to evade telegraphed red attacks and rely on normal positioning or well-timed blocks for standard incoming strikes.

### Can I reset my allocated stat points later in the game?
Yes, players can reset their attribute distribution by obtaining curriculum realignment tokens from campus vendor milestones or special event bounties. However, because these reset tokens are limited in the early stages, following our balanced stat distribution guide is recommended.
