*   **Shadow Casting:** Lanterns are fixed at eye level. Check that the shadows cast by the weapon racks match their physical geometry. Elongated or moving shadows indicate an active Class IV spectral presence.

## Common Pitfalls and Survival Tactics

Many community reports highlight that skipping steps outlined in this **jujutsu school anomaly inspection guide** leads directly to run-ending ambushes. Developing healthy inspection habits will drastically reduce your deaths and prevent frustration during extended sessions.

### Relying Exclusively on Flashlights

While your flashlight is essential in unlit sectors like the Basement Storage Corridor, over-reliance can blind you to ambient luminescence. Certain Class I anomalies—such as ghostly handprints or faint cursed runes—are only visible under low ambient light or ultraviolet conditions. Toggle your flashlight periodically when scanning bare walls.

### Overlooking Audio Clues

Visual checks comprise only part of your inspection duties. Sound design plays an integral role in signaling spatial distortion. If you hear the distinctive patter of bare feet on tatami mats while traversing wooden decking, the game has triggered an auditory anomaly. Always play with spatial stereo headphones to accurately pinpoint the direction and distance of acoustic irregularities.

### Panicking During Hostile Chases

When a Class V cursed entity spawns, panic often drives players to make sudden, erratic turns that trap them against dead ends. Keep the layout of the current wing visualized in your mind. If an entity manifests directly ahead, perform an immediate 180-degree turn and sprint cleanly back toward the safety zone. Never hide inside closets or side rooms unless the specific entity mechanic demands breaking line of sight.

### What should I do first if I spot an anomaly?
As soon as you spot an irregularity, stop advancing immediately. Audio cues frequently reveal anomalies before visual signs manifest. You may hear muted whispers, distant bells, metallic scraping, or delayed footsteps behind your character.
