### What should I do if my loop counter resets unexpectedly?
An unexpected loop reset means you either marched forward past an active anomaly or turned back on a completely normal hallway (a false positive). Take a breath, reset your baseline observations, and re-check subtle environmental elements such as clock hands, lighting hues, and wall flyers.

### How do I know if an anomaly is lethal?
Anomalies featuring active humanoid silhouettes, red eye clusters, or rapid auditory heartbeats are typically lethal upon close contact. Environmental anomalies—like inverted text, missing trash cans, or distorted pictures—are harmless if you simply turn around and walk back through the entry door.
